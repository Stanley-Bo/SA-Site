About Shugar Prestige

A new chapter at Paragon, built around your treatments.

Shugar Prestige is entering a new chapter at Paragon — refreshed, and focused on doing a few things very well: nail spa, facial, lash and brow work, hair removal, and gentle spa treatments for little ones.

Every visit is built around what your skin, nails or schedule actually need that day — not a fixed package. We use trusted names like Payot for facials and O.P.I for nail care, and Kangen ionised water throughout our facial treatments.

The Little Princess experience

A pampering afternoon made just for children — gentle, playful, and every bit as considered as our adult menu. Little ones get their own full-size chair, an iPad and headphones, and a treatment paced to keep them comfortable from start to finish.

  • Little Princess Manicure & Pedicure — soft shaping, buffing and child-safe polish for little hands and feet.
  • Little Princess Facial — a gentle customised facial finished with our signature chocolate mask.
  • Mother & daughter side-by-side — book two chairs together and make it a shared ritual.

Our experienced team looks forward to receiving you — and your little one.
