Our Story

Beauty as a Calling, Not Just a Career

Shugar Prestige was born from a lifetime of dedication to beauty. Our founder, Ms Jenny Ho, opened Classic Beauty Care in 1995 — building a loyal following over more than two decades through her genuine commitment to every client's wellbeing.

In March 2018, Ms Ho and her daughter Lynn Lim elevated the brand and launched Shugar Prestige at The Paragon — Singapore's most iconic shopping destination. Together, they brought a new vision: a beautiful, feminine, and technically exceptional salon where every woman feels seen, heard, and truly cared for.

  • Payot Paris skincare products for all facial treatments
  • Kangen ionised water for superior skin penetration
  • World-class equipment sourced from international research trips
  • Organic, herbal nail spa — first of its kind in Singapore
  • No hard selling — your comfort and trust come first
