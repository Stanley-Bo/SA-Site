Frequently asked

How long does the visit take?

Around ninety minutes from start to finish — roughly forty-five for the Lumi Laser facial and forty-five for the Gelish manicure, including a short consultation when you arrive.

How should I prepare beforehand?

Come with bare skin if you can, or we’ll cleanse for you. Avoid retinol, acids and scrubs for three days before, and strong sun or tanning for a week. Remove any existing gel or extensions with us rather than at home, and tell us about recent peels, medication or breakouts when you book.

What is the aftercare?

Skin may look faintly flushed for an hour. Keep it simple for twenty-four hours — gentle cleanser, moisturiser, no acids, scrubs, saunas or heavy make-up — and wear SPF daily for the week after. For nails, avoid harsh cleaning products without gloves, oil your cuticles nightly, and come back to us for removal rather than peeling the colour off.

Is the facial suitable for sensitive skin?

In most cases, yes. We assess your skin at the start and adjust the treatment — do mention any active breakouts, recent peels or medication when you book.

Can I add nail art or extensions?

Of course. Art and extensions sit outside the promotional price and are quoted at the chair.

How long will the Gelish manicure last?

Two to three weeks of high shine with normal wear, longer if you oil your cuticles and wear gloves for housework.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us at least twenty-four hours’ notice to reschedule or cancel. Arriving more than fifteen minutes late may mean we shorten or move part of the package. The offer is by appointment, one promotion per visit, and not combinable with other packages or member rates.

Can I gift it to someone?

Yes — message us and we’ll arrange a gift card for the package.
